How SkillSakhi Works

A simple ecosystem to share knowledge and empower each other.

1. Teach

Share your expertise in coding, design, languages, or life skills with other women.

2. Earn Credits

For every hour you teach, earn Skill Credits to your wallet.

3. Learn

Spend your credits to book sessions and learn new skills from experts.
